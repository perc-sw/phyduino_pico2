Checked and OK

  • AP63200 feedback dividers: 3.33 V (196k/62k) and 4.99 V (157k/30k)
  • TPS25947 eFuses: limit ≈ 3 A each (RILM 1.1 kΩ); windows ≈ 4.5–22.4 V (barrel/shield) and 4.5–5.6 V (USB); true reverse-current blocking makes the OR'd outputs valid
  • TPS2121 pin mapping, highest-voltage mode, OV dividers (≈ 5.53 V), ILIM ≈ 2.6 A
  • RP2350 core regulator: 3.3 µH inductor, 4.7 µF on VREG_VIN and DVDD, RC filter on VREG_AVDD
  • Decoupling on IOVDD, DVDD and ADC_AVDD (ferrite + 100 nF)
  • USB: 27 Ω series resistors, 5.1 kΩ CC resistors, ESD array, shield RC
  • SWD connector pinout matches the Raspberry Pi 3-pin debug connector
  • Arduino mapping: SPI1 on D10–D13, UART0 on D0/D1, I²C1 on D14/D15
  • HSTX lanes on GPIO12–19 with ground between pairs on J7
  • ADS7038 on SPI0; I²C0 pull-ups 5.1 kΩ; EEPROM 0x50 and expander 0x43 (no address conflict)
  • Boot-select slide switch acting on flash CS through R49
  • Shared QSPI bus between flash and PSRAM (apart from finding #4)
Sources: ESP-AT ESP32-C6 hardware connection · Torex XC6190 datasheet · TI TPS25947 datasheet · RP2350, TPS2121, ESP32-C6-MINI-1 and AP63200 datasheets in the repository.
