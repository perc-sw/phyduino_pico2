Checked and OK

  • Stackup matches JLC04161H-7628: 0.2104 / 1.065 / 0.2104 mm, 35 / 15.2 µm copper, prepreg εr 4.4, core 4.6, mask 3.8 at 0.015 mm (#5); a 50 Ω single-ended trace works out at about 0.35 mm, matching JLC's figure
  • USB pair 0.30 / 0.15 mm on every segment: about 92 Ω without mask, 88–90 Ω with it, within 90 Ω ±10% (JLC guide) and ±15% (USB 2.0) (#1)
  • USB P/N mismatch 1.5 mm on USB_IO (about 9 ps); at J6, 0.9 or 2.7 mm depending on plug orientation, under the JLC guide's 200 mil
  • J6 data pads joined at the pad row on F.Cu with no vias; stubs 0–2.7 mm, under the JLC guide's 200 mil (#8)
  • USB path has 2 layer changes in total (the USB_IO vias), within the JLC guide's maximum of 2
  • B.Cu sections of every USB and HSTX pair run over solid In2 GND; F.Cu USB sections run over continuous +3.3V on In1, with no split crossings (#6)
  • The USB_IO layer changes have +3.3V-to-GND capacitors within 2–3 mm (C129 / C125 100 nF at R3/R4, C3 4.7 µF at U1) to carry the return current between planes (#4)
  • HSTX pairs 0.25 / 0.20 mm: about 105 Ω without mask, about 101 Ω with it; P/N mismatch about 0.7 mm per lane; lanes 53.4–57.6 mm long (#3)
  • HSTX lanes at least 0.3 mm apart outside U1 (0.23 mm for 2.5 mm below U1); 0.15 mm under U1 accepted (#3)
  • FLASH_CLK at least 0.655 mm from the USB pair (#7)
  • VBUS detect: R151 22 kΩ / R152 33 kΩ from +V_USB to GPIO34 (USB VBUS DET function, fault-tolerant), top resistor near C27, 25.6 mm F.Cu trace with no vias (#2)
  • ESD array U9 about 3 mm from J6, ahead of the 27 Ω resistors, with no stubs between them; R3/R4 placement accepted for full speed (#9)
  • CC1 and CC2 each have their own 5.1 kΩ Rd (R48, R47) close to J6
  • VBUS capacitance at the connector: C27 10 µF is at, not over, the USB 2.0 / Type-C cSnkBulk limit (schematic #12)
  • Inrush through U13: C140 3.3 nF gives about 0.55 V/ms (TPS25947 table), about 120 mA into the ~220 µF on +VIN_REG
  • U13 start 4.47 V / cut-out 4.06 V / over-voltage 5.58 V: still above the 5.5 V Type-C maximum; start threshold accepted in schematic #13
  • U13 and U6 block reverse current, so the barrel or shield supply can't drive USB VBUS
  • HSTX lane 0/1 J7-end vias have C137 100 nF within 1.0–3.6 mm; C127/C128 add return paths near R3/R4 and U9 (#4)
  • Zones refilled: DRC gives the same 68 items with or without a refill (16:46 save)
  • DRC: 0 unconnected items and schematic parity clean; the 68 remaining items are text, silkscreen and footprint-library warnings outside this review, plus 6 exclusions (J6 GND pads to its NPTH locating holes, J1 mounting pins to the board edge) that are part of the footprints
